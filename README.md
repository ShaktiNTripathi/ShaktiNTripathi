### About

I am a full-stack software engineer with more than **seven years** in the information technology sector since **2019**. I design and deliver reliable web platforms, SaaS products, and WhatsApp Cloud API integrations for healthcare, CRM, e-commerce, and financial use cases.

My work emphasizes clear architecture, security, and maintainable code: **Laravel**, **Angular**, **Node.js**, **MySQL**, **AWS**, and **Docker**, with strong attention to authentication, billing, webhooks, and role-based access.

I hold **CCNA** and **CEH** credentials and apply security best practices throughout the development lifecycle. I have contributed **3,000+** engineering deliveries across **50+** products and modules.
